Built to be trusted

An assistant with access to your mail should have firm rules. These are Heed's.

They're enforced in code, not left to the AI.

Nothing is sent without your tap

There is no auto-send setting, and there never will be. Every reply waits for you.

The AI never picks who gets a reply

Recipients come from the original email's headers. An email can't trick Heed into writing to someone else.

When unsure, it leaves mail alone

If Heed isn't confident, or the AI is down, the email stays unread and untouched for you.

Never deletes, archives or marks spam

Handled mail is marked read and labelled. It stays exactly where it was in Gmail.

Summaries, not your mailbox

Heed reads email bodies from Gmail when it needs them and doesn't keep them. Summaries expire after 30 days.

Your mail doesn't train anyone's AI

Heed only uses AI providers that don't keep or train on what they're sent.

Heed's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect an account, or delete everything, at any time from the app or the delete account page.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Something else? Write to support@heed.reactmode.dev.

Will Heed ever send an email by itself?

No. Heed writes drafts; only you can send them, by tapping Send. Even then you have five seconds to undo.

What happens to the emails Heed reads?

They're marked read and labelled Heed/Handled in Gmail, and stay in your inbox. Nothing is deleted, archived or moved to spam. You can mark any of them unread again from the app.

What does Heed store?

A one-line summary and basic details of each email (sender, subject, time), for 30 days. Email bodies are read from Gmail when needed and aren't kept.

Which email providers does it work with?

Gmail, up to five accounts per person. Other providers may come later.

What if Heed gets something wrong?

Every handled email can be undone, and you can tell Heed to never auto-read a sender again. When Heed isn't sure about an email, it leaves it unread instead of guessing.

How do I remove Heed's access?

Disconnect an account in the app, or delete your Heed account. Both revoke Heed's access to Google straight away and remove what Heed stored.
